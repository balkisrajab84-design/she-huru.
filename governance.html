Governance & Accountability

Built on Trust, Transparency and Integrity

Strong governance is the foundation of lasting impact. Learn about our leadership structure, accountability mechanisms, and the policies that guide our work.

Our Governance Structure

She Huru Initiative operates under a dual-leadership model that separates strategic oversight from day-to-day management. This ensures checks and balances while allowing our team to remain agile and responsive to community needs.

  • Board of Management: Provides strategic guidance, fiduciary oversight, and policy approval. Meets quarterly.
  • Executive Leadership: Manages daily operations, programme implementation, and staff well-being.
  • Community Advisory Council: Ensures that our work remains grounded in the lived realities and priorities of the LBQ and GNC community.

Financial Stewardship

We are deeply committed to responsible financial management. Every shilling entrusted to us is treated as a resource for the community.

  • Annual external audits by independent accounting firms.
  • Clear financial policies, procurement guidelines, and internal controls.
  • Regular financial reporting to donors and partners.
  • Transparent budgeting processes overseen by the Board.
