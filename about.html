About She Huru

A Community-Led Feminist Movement Rooted in Mombasa

Since 2022, She Huru Initiative has worked alongside lesbian, bisexual, queer and gender non-conforming communities to build safety, dignity, healing and economic justice along Kenya's coast.

Our Story

Born from Community, Built for Community

She Huru Initiative was founded in 2022 by a small group of queer women and gender-diverse organizers in Mombasa who saw that existing services rarely reflected their lived realities. What began as informal peer support — conversations, safe spaces, and mutual aid — grew into a registered community-based organization.

Today, we work across seven thematic areas: economic justice, healing justice and mental health, gender-based violence prevention and response, leadership development, sexual and reproductive health and rights, movement building, and advocacy and human rights.

We remain anchored in the belief that communities most affected by injustice are best placed to lead solutions — and that sustainable change is built through collective care.

Who We Are

She Huru Initiative is a community-led queer feminist organization based in Mombasa, Kenya, founded in 2022, advancing safety, dignity, economic justice, healing, leadership and human rights for LBQ and gender non-conforming persons.

Our Compass

Mission, Vision & Approach

The principles that guide every programme, partnership and decision we make.

Our Mission

To advance safety, dignity, economic justice, healing, leadership and human rights for lesbian, bisexual, queer and gender non-conforming persons through advocacy, community organizing, mental health support, movement building and economic empowerment.

Our Vision

A Kenya where lesbian, bisexual, queer and gender non-conforming persons live free from violence, discrimination and exclusion — with full access to safety, dignity, opportunity and justice.

Our Approach

Peer-led, survivor-centered, trauma-informed and community-owned. We invest in the leadership of people most affected by injustice, and we build partnerships rooted in mutual respect and shared accountability.

Theory of Change

How Change Happens

Our work is grounded in a clear theory of how individual, community and systemic change reinforce one another.

01

Heal & Support

We create safe, accessible spaces for psychosocial support, peer connection and healing — so that community members can live with dignity and safety.

02

Build Power

We invest in leadership development, economic empowerment and organizing skills — so that community members can shape their own futures.

03

Change Systems

We advocate for policy, practice and cultural change — so that the systems around our communities protect rather than harm them.

Our Journey

Organizational Growth

From a small circle of organizers to a recognized community-led organization.

2022

She Huru Initiative is founded in Mombasa by queer women and gender-diverse organizers, initially as an informal peer support network.

2023

Formal registration as a community-based organization; first structured programmes in mental health and economic justice launched.

2024

Expansion of GBV response and SRHR programming; first strategic partnerships formed with regional and national organizations.

2025

Leadership development cohort launched; advocacy work scaled across coastal counties.

2026

Development of a Vision 2030 strategic plan; continued growth in institutional partnerships and community reach.
