Your Impact

What Your Donation Supports

Every contribution, no matter the size, goes directly toward sustaining our community-led programmes.

💚

Healing & Mental Health

KES 2,500 covers a peer support session for 10 community members, providing a safe space for healing and connection.

🛡️

GBV Response

KES 5,000 helps provide emergency transport and referral support for a survivor of gender-based violence.

🚀

Leadership Development

KES 10,000 supports a young queer leader to attend a week-long organizing and advocacy training.

Our Commitment to Transparency

We know that trust is earned. She Huru Initiative is committed to the highest standards of financial accountability. We undergo regular audits and report transparently to our donors. 100% of your donation goes directly to supporting our community programmes and operational sustainability in Mombasa. If you have any questions about how funds are used, please do not hesitate to contact us.
