Our Programmes

Seven Thematic Areas, One Shared Vision

Each of our programmes responds to a distinct dimension of the lived realities of LBQ and gender non-conforming communities along Kenya's coast — and together, they form an integrated model for change.

💼
Programme 01

Economic Justice

Purpose

To build sustainable livelihoods and financial independence for LBQ and gender non-conforming community members, reducing economic vulnerability and dependence on unsafe environments.

Problem Statement

Many community members face exclusion from formal employment, discrimination in the workplace, and limited access to credit, skills or markets — which deepens poverty and increases exposure to violence and exploitation.

Our Approach

We combine skills training, small business support, savings groups and peer mentorship, delivered through a queer-affirming and trauma-informed lens.

Key Activities

  • Vocational and entrepreneurial skills workshops
  • Community savings and lending circles
  • Business mentorship and start-up grants (where funding allows)
  • Financial literacy and rights education

Impact

Community members have launched small enterprises, joined savings groups, and reported greater control over their incomes and decisions. Numbers will be updated as verified data becomes available.

Programme 02

Mental Health & Healing

Purpose

To create accessible, community-based psychosocial support and healing spaces that center safety, dignity and peer care.

Problem Statement

Queer and gender non-conforming persons experience elevated rates of anxiety, depression, trauma and isolation, often compounded by family rejection, violence and stigma — while mainstream mental health services remain inaccessible, unaffordable or unsafe.

Our Approach

We offer peer-led support groups, referrals to affirming counsellors, and healing-centered community events that build resilience through connection rather than pathology.

Key Activities

  • Peer support circles and safe spaces
  • Referrals to queer-affirming mental health professionals
  • Healing and wellness workshops
  • Psychoeducation on trauma, stress and self-care

Impact

Community members report reduced isolation, stronger peer networks and improved access to affirming care. Detailed impact data will be published as it is verified.

Programme 03

Gender-Based Violence Prevention & Response

Purpose

To prevent gender-based violence and provide survivor-centered, confidential response and referral support for LBQ and gender non-conforming persons.

Problem Statement

Community members face heightened risk of intimate partner violence, sexual violence, "corrective" violence, family rejection and community violence — yet often cannot safely report to mainstream services due to discrimination or fear of exposure.

Our Approach

We work through trusted peer responders, provide confidential referral pathways, and engage in community-level prevention and awareness work.

Key Activities

  • Confidential referral and accompaniment support
  • Peer responder training
  • Community awareness and prevention dialogues
  • Advocacy for survivor-centered services

Impact

Survivors have accessed referral services and accompaniment support through trusted community pathways. Verified figures will be published as available.

Programme 04

Sexual & Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR)

Purpose

To advance the sexual and reproductive health and rights of LBQ and gender non-conforming persons through information, access and advocacy.

Problem Statement

Community members frequently encounter judgment, misinformation, or outright denial of services when seeking SRHR care — resulting in unmet health needs and preventable harm.

Our Approach

We provide accurate, non-judgmental information, build referral relationships with affirming providers, and advocate for inclusive SRHR policy and practice.

Key Activities

  • Community SRHR education sessions
  • Referrals to affirming health providers
  • Provider sensitization and engagement
  • Advocacy for inclusive health policy

Impact

Community members have been connected to affirming SRHR information and services, and providers have been engaged on inclusive practice. Verified figures will be published as available.

Programme 05

Leadership Development

Purpose

To invest in the next generation of queer feminist leaders through mentorship, skills-building and organizing opportunities.

Problem Statement

Community leadership is often unrecognized and under-resourced, and emerging leaders face barriers to formal training, networks and visibility.

Our Approach

We offer structured cohorts, mentorship, and hands-on organizing experience that centers lived experience as expertise.

Key Activities

  • Leadership cohorts and mentorship
  • Organizing and advocacy skills training
  • Peer exchange and networking
  • Support for community-led initiatives

Impact

Emerging leaders have taken on organizing roles within and beyond She Huru, strengthening the broader movement ecosystem.

Programme 06

Movement Building

Purpose

To connect, strengthen and amplify movements for justice across Kenya and the region.

Problem Statement

Movements are often fragmented, under-resourced and isolated — reducing collective power and limiting the impact of advocacy.

Our Approach

We convene, co-create and partner with other community-led organizations to build shared strategy, solidarity and collective voice.

Key Activities

  • Convenings and movement dialogues
  • Coalition and network participation
  • Joint advocacy initiatives
  • Solidarity and mutual aid

Impact

Stronger relationships and joint advocacy across community-led organizations, contributing to a more connected and resilient movement.

Programme 07

Advocacy & Human Rights

Purpose

To advance the human rights of LBQ and gender non-conforming persons through evidence-based advocacy, policy engagement and public education.

Problem Statement

Laws, policies and social norms continue to expose community members to discrimination, violence and exclusion, with limited avenues for safe, effective advocacy.

Our Approach

We combine community evidence, partnerships and strategic engagement to advance rights-based change at local and national levels.

Key Activities

  • Policy analysis and engagement
  • Community evidence and documentation
  • Public education and awareness
  • Coalition-based advocacy

Impact

Increased visibility of community priorities in policy spaces, and stronger alliances for rights-based advocacy.
