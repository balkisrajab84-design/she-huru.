Who We Are

A Movement Rooted in Community, Led by Community

Founded in 2022 in Mombasa, She Huru Initiative is a community-led queer feminist organization working at the intersection of healing, safety, and economic justice for lesbian, bisexual, queer and gender non-conforming persons along Kenya's coast.

We believe that lasting change is built by the communities most affected — through peer support, leadership development, advocacy, and collective care.

Learn more about us →

Our Mission

To advance safety, dignity, economic justice, healing, leadership and human rights for lesbian, bisexual, queer and gender non-conforming persons through advocacy, community organizing, mental health support, movement building and economic empowerment.
